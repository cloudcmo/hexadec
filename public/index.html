How it goes

Sixteen tiles. Hidden among them are four four-letter words that use every tile exactly. Build one and it lands on the next row down.

  • Plenty of words can be made. Only some sets of four use up all sixteen tiles, so three good words can still leave you holding nonsense.
  • Tap any letter of a word already on the grid to take it back. The words below it move up.
  • Columns that happen to read as a word score a bonus too, top to bottom. A full four-letter column scores double.
  • Finish quickly and you keep what is left of five quiet minutes as bonus points. There is no clock on screen.
2×L double letter 3×L triple letter 2×W double word 3×W triple word

A premium square counts on the turn you cover it, and it counts for the column too. Later words score their own squares, not yours.
