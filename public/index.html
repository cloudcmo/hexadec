How to play

Sixteen tiles. Hidden among them are four four-letter words that use every tile exactly. Tap tiles and they go straight onto the highlighted row; tap Place word to put the word down.

  • Plenty of words can be made. Only some sets of four use up all sixteen tiles, so three good words can still leave you holding nonsense.
  • The highest empty row is highlighted. Tap any other empty row to put your word there instead.
  • Tap a word already on the grid to take it back. The others stay where they are.
  • Columns that happen to read as a word score a bonus too, top to bottom. A full four-letter column scores double. Columns read from the top row, so they count once every row above them is filled.
  • Finish inside five quiet minutes and you keep a point for every five seconds left, up to 60. There is no clock on screen.
  • Properly stuck? I'm stuck finds you a word that still leaves a way home. Once per game, and it costs 20 points.
  • Drag the tiles in your hand to rearrange them. A quick tap still picks one.
  • On a keyboard you can simply type the word. Backspace takes a letter back, enter places it, escape clears, and the up and down arrows move between empty rows.
